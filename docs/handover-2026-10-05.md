## 0. The one hard rule

The repo has two halves. **Paul owns the dogs side. Julie (GitHub `lalules9`)
owns the staff app.** Never edit the staff side without checking with Julie
first and having a very good reason:

- `src/app/shift/**`, `src/components/shift/**`
- `src/lib/shift*.ts`, `leave*.ts`, `care-data.ts`
- `src/lib/actions/{shift,shift-people,leave,care}.ts`
- `src/app/api/shift-checks`, `src/app/approve/leave`
- `public/shift-manifest.json`, `docs/staff-app-plan.md`
- migrations 22–40 (shift, leave, care, guest sign-in)

Staff, their roster and their shift history are managed in the staff app. Both
apps share one database: `people`, `person_roles`, `volunteer_profile`,
`org_settings` and the roster/task tables. Those are the overlap points.
Deleting a `people` row cascades to shift history, so the dogs side refuses to
delete, merge away or archive anyone with a staff/admin role or any shift record
(`staffProtection()` in `src/lib/actions/people.ts`). A database-level guard is
still an open item to agree with Julie.

## 1. Working agreements (Paul's rules)

- Branch off `origin/main`, open a PR, and **ask before every push/merge**.
  Merging deploys to production (Vercel auto-deploys `main`). Local `main` is
  often stale — always `git fetch` and branch from `origin/main`.
- Check `tsc --noEmit` and `next lint` before pushing. Skip a local
  `next build` (slow on this Windows machine); Vercel builds it.
- Reuse the words staff already know. Don't invent new button labels.
- Test data uses obviously fictional names (celebrity dogs). Delete the seed
  data before real data goes in.
- Don't change a page Paul is testing mid-session — edits reset his place.
  Collect his notes and make them in one batch.
- Never put the shared PIN, API keys or the Google client secret in the repo
  (it is public).

## 2. What is live (all on `main`)

**Dogs:** status-grouped list (Available / Out now filters), dog detail,
one-tap Start Walk / End X (green start, blue end, red only when overdue),
⋯ **Start activity** menu (Yard, Bed Rest, Jail Break, Foster, Manual entry),
Manual entry with an Activity dropdown for every type, Edit times, yard
"due back in…" or "at…", "days from now" shortcut, Notes (not Reason).

**Site Visitors** (was "Site"): sign in / out board (orange sign-out), caretakers
excluded from the picker (staff role without admin), link to Logs and to the
printable QR page.

**People:** list with role badges and multi-select role chips, nickname format,
walker last-walk / 4-week time, Awaiting-approval roll-down, person page with a
single **Update status** menu (Volunteer +, Committee for admins; Add jail break /
foster carer for staff), edit, archive/restore, merge, delete (admin). Staff and
admin rows are read-only here.

**Approvals:** only people named in `org_settings.approver_person_ids`
(Paul, Julie, Shayna — migration 42; falls back to any admin until applied) can
approve or decline. Foster needs a passing home check. Jail break can also be
approved from the one-click emailed link.

**Sign-in:** emailed code and Google. Only admin, staff, committee and
Volunteer + may sign in; general volunteers are checked in by a caretaker and
are refused with a message. A fresh sign-in leaves volunteer mode.

**Modes:** staff mode ↔ volunteer mode (buttons "Switch to Volunteer Mode" /
"Switch to Staff Mode"). One shared PIN (4–6 digits) is stored hashed in
`org_settings`; admins change it in ⚙ Settings without needing the old one;
5 wrong tries pauses 5 minutes. Ask Paul for the current PIN — never commit it.

**The one public form** (`/apply/volunteer`; short links `/volunteer` and
`/homecare`; QR page `/apply/qr`): page 1 "Work with us" → interests (volunteer,
jail break, fostering, adoption) + email and surname. Registered people only give
new details (home/garden for homecare). New people get short pages: About you,
Emergency contact, You and dogs, Your home (homecare only), Finish up (image
consent, optional photo, terms, signature). Under-18s get a parent/guardian block
and the parent gets a consent email. Adoption records a pending `adopter` role
and emails CAPS. Progress is kept in sessionStorage; browser Back/Forward move
between pages.

**Logs:** All activity tab (default) with type chips, type-to-search Dog/Person,
status, flags, notes search, quick date presets, totals; Medical, Visitors,
Dogs, People registers; CSV export. (The per-type tabs were removed on 7 Oct; the
chips do that job.) Day boundaries use UTC+10 (Weipa).

**Help tab:** searchable how-to guides, role-aware (`src/lib/help-content.ts`).
**Bottom nav** has icons. **Reports:** needs-a-walk, currently-out, homecare
load, length of stay.
